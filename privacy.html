1. What Information Is Collected

Caveman Dan has no account or login system and no user-generated content. We do not collect your name, email address, physical location, contacts, photos, or payment information. The App does not offer in-app purchases.

The App does use third-party services (Google AdMob for advertising and Firebase Analytics) that collect limited technical data on their own behalf and ours, as summarized below. This mirrors the "data used to track you" and "data linked to you" categories disclosed in the App Store's Privacy Nutrition Label for Caveman Dan.

2. How Information Is Used

  • Displaying ads: Google AdMob serves banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads within the App. When permitted, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to personalize ads and measure ad performance.
  • Understanding app usage: Firebase Analytics helps us understand how players interact with the game (e.g., levels played, session length, crashes) so we can improve it. This data is not used to identify you personally.
  • Ad attribution: The App uses Apple's SKAdNetwork framework to measure the effectiveness of ad campaigns in a privacy-preserving way. SKAdNetwork identifiers for our advertising partners' networks (including Meta and Google) may be included in the App's configuration to enable this attribution without revealing user-level data to us.
  • Game Center leaderboard: If you choose to participate in the App's daily leaderboard, this feature is provided and governed by Apple's Game Center. We do not receive or store your Game Center data — see Section 3.

4. App Tracking Transparency & Advertising Identifiers

On iOS, Caveman Dan requests permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before any tracking-related data (such as the IDFA — Identifier for Advertisers) is used. You will see a system prompt asking whether the App may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites.

  • If you allow tracking, AdMob may use your device's IDFA to deliver more personalized ads and measure ad campaign performance.
  • If you decline tracking, the App will still display ads, but they will be less personalized (contextual, non-tracking-based advertising), and AdMob will not use the IDFA for tracking purposes.

You can change your tracking preference at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or under the specific app entry in that menu.

5. GDPR / EEA & UK Rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and equivalent UK data protection law apply to certain processing described in this policy.

Legal Bases for Processing

  • Consent — for personalized advertising and tracking-related data uses (including IDFA-based tracking), we rely on your consent, collected through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form shown to EEA/UK users, and through the ATT prompt on iOS.
  • Legitimate interests — for basic app analytics and non-personalized advertising necessary to keep the free App running, we and our partners may rely on legitimate interests, balanced against your rights.

UMP Consent Message

Users in the EEA and UK are shown a consent message (via Google's UMP SDK) before any non-essential data processing for advertising purposes begins. You can review or withdraw your consent choices at any time by revisiting the consent form, where available in the App's settings, or by adjusting your device's ATT and ad tracking settings.

Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to: access the data held about you; request correction or deletion; object to or restrict certain processing; request data portability; and withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing. Because we do not maintain user accounts and do not store personal data on our own servers, most of these requests are best directed to Google (as the processor of AdMob/Firebase data) or fulfilled by adjusting your device's tracking and consent settings. You may also contact us using the details in Section 11, and we will assist where we are able, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

6. CCPA / CPRA (California) Rights

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), gives you certain rights regarding personal information, including the right to know what categories of personal information are collected, the right to request deletion, the right to correct inaccurate information, and the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (including for cross-context behavioral advertising).

We do not sell personal information for money. Ad personalization through AdMob may be considered "sharing" under CPRA. You can opt out by declining App Tracking Transparency permission on iOS and/or adjusting your device's "Limit Ad Tracking" / ad personalization settings, and via the consent tools referenced in Section 5. To exercise any CCPA/CPRA rights, contact us using the details in Section 11.

7. Data Retention

We do not operate servers that store your personal data. Game Center data is retained by Apple according to Apple's own policies.

8. Children's Privacy

Caveman Dan is not directed at children under the age of 13, and the App's intended age rating is approximately 9+/12+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, please contact us at the email address below so we can address it, including coordinating removal where applicable. This App is not designed to comply with, and should not be treated as being directed toward, audiences requiring child-specific consent frameworks such as COPPA-covered services.
