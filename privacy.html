1. What Information Is Collected

Caveman Dan has no account or login system, in-app chat, or user-generated-content feed. Saved game progress, preferences and local diagnostic counters are stored on your device. The App does not request your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or payment information to play, and it has no in-app purchases. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send.

The App uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising and applicable advertising choices. Separately, Share usage analytics in Settings is optional and off by default. If you enable it, Google Analytics for Firebase receives usage information to help improve the game. Approximate location can be derived from network information; this is different from accessing your precise location through iOS Location Services.

2. How Information Is Used

  • Displaying ads: AdMob serves banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Ad requests depend on applicable advertising choices and availability. Declining tracking or optional Analytics does not block gameplay.
  • Understanding app usage: After a separate Analytics opt-in, Firebase helps us measure sessions, game outcomes, interactions and ad performance so we can improve the game. Analytics advertising storage, advertising user-data use and ad personalization are disabled in this integration.
  • Ad attribution: Apple's SKAdNetwork can measure advertising effectiveness. Network identifiers in the App's configuration do not themselves mean that the App operates a social account or sends your game progress to that network.
  • Local progress: The current release keeps progress on the device. It does not provide an active Game Center leaderboard or cloud progress sync.
  • Support: Information you voluntarily email is used to respond to your request.

4. App Tracking Transparency & Advertising Identifiers

Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission is separate from optional usage Analytics and regional advertising consent. Where eligible, the App offers an explanation before requesting the system tracking permission; it does not require tracking to play.

  • If you allow tracking and other applicable permissions permit, AdMob may use the advertising identifier for personalized advertising and measurement.
  • If you decline, are restricted, or have not authorized tracking, this app requests non-personalized ads. This does not mean advertising SDKs collect no technical information.

You can change the tracking choice in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Where UMP requires an entry point, Privacy Options on the App's Home screen reopens advertising choices. The App refreshes its permission state and discards preloaded ads when these choices change.

Optional usage Analytics

Open the App's Settings > Share usage analytics to enable or disable Firebase usage measurement. The setting starts off; agreeing to ATT or a UMP advertising form does not enable it. Turning it off stops app event forwarding and SDK Analytics collection and clears Analytics stored on this device. It does not delete information already sent to Google. Re-enabling starts a new local Analytics collection period; it does not upload the history of local QA counters. For previously sent information or other privacy requests, use the contact details below.

5. GDPR / EEA & UK Rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, applicable data-protection law can provide rights over your information.

Consent and advertising choices

Optional usage Analytics relies on your separate, default-off in-app choice. Where required, advertising consent is gathered through Google's UMP; ATT supplies Apple's additional tracking permission. The advertising form explains applicable purposes and choices. Technical processing needed to operate the consent service and any permitted limited advertising follows the applicable service practices and legal requirements; refusing personalized ads is not permission for unrelated Analytics.

Where a UMP privacy-options entry point is required, use Home > Privacy Options to revisit advertising choices. Use Settings > Share usage analytics for optional Analytics. These controls have different purposes, and either can be changed without losing access to gameplay.

Your rights

7. Data Retention

Saved game progress and preferences remain on your device until removed through app/device controls or deletion of the App, subject to any device backups you maintain. Turning optional Analytics off clears its local Analytics data, not saved game progress.

Our Google Analytics property is configured to retain event-level data for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months. New user activity resets the user-data retention period. These settings do not govern most standard reports based on aggregated data. Information collected by AdMob and UMP follows those services' applicable retention practices. We use those records for the purposes described above and review retention against those purposes and applicable requirements. Provider information is linked in Section 3. We do not promise that uninstalling the App, resetting local Analytics or changing ATT instantly deletes records already received by a provider.

Contact us to request applicable access or deletion, including the game/device details relevant to your request. We will review it and coordinate with the relevant provider; service retention rules and lawful exceptions may affect what can be deleted. We retain support correspondence as needed to respond, resolve the issue and meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. Do not send passwords or advertising identifiers in an initial support email.
